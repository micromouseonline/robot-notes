# Micromouse Wall Sensors


!!! Note "Outline"

    This section aims to cover all aspects of the design, implementation and operation of the wall sensors in a micromouse robot.

    Once you understand what is required of the wall sensors, you can look at how to achieve that aim.

    We will look at some of the circuit options with their associated benefits and drawbacks.

    Along tghe way there will be some practical circuits, tested on the bench along with enough theory to help you make better decisions about how to design your own sensor assemblies and circuits.
